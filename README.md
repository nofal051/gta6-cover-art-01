Turn a photo and a caption into GTA 6 cover art. Vice Nights Cover Press
offers 4 layouts, 5 colour grades, photo/panel pan-and-zoom, a caption
editor, and PNG export in 3 sizes. Ships with 3 procedural sample photos;
fonts are Anton and Pacifico (OFL). Runs fully offline in the browser.
