Vice Nights Cover Press turns one of your photos and a line of text into
a poster that looks like it belongs on a Vice City billboard. Pick a
layout, pick a colour grade, drag the photos into place, write your
caption, and download the finished cover as a PNG.

## What it does

- **5 layouts** — Hero + Three, a 2 × 2 grid, one full-bleed photo,
  three vertical strips, and Duo Split: two photos side by side sharing
  one caption bar along the bottom.
- **6 colour grades** — Sunset, Neon Night, Golden Hour, Dusk Violet,
  Palm Noir, and the new Vice Dusk, a purple-and-teal duotone with twin
  corner glows.
- **Photo and panel pan + zoom** — click a panel to select it, drag its
  photo to pan, scroll to zoom, or use the per-panel zoom slider. Load
  your own photos or use the three procedural sample photos that ship
  with the app (a boulevard sunset, a neon street, a pier at golden
  hour).
- **Caption system** — five one-tap caption presets (Last Score,
  Boulevard Queen, Neon Shore, Clean Getaway, Vice Dusk), each with its
  own title, subtitle, keyword chips, size and shadow; plus a caption
  size switch (Small / Medium / Large) and a text-shadow toggle for a
  flat print-poster look. Typing your own caption clears the preset
  highlight but keeps your size and shadow choices.
- **PNG export in 3 sizes** — Square 1080 × 1080, Portrait
  1080 × 1350, and Wide 1920 × 1080, rendered at full export size from
  the same painter the preview uses, so what you see is what you get.

## Fonts and offline use

The lettering uses Anton for the big title and Pacifico for the
handwritten subtitle, both bundled under the SIL Open Font License (see
`app/fonts/OFL.txt`). The app runs fully offline in the browser: no
uploads, no accounts, no network calls. Everything you make stays on
your machine.

## Try it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open <http://localhost:8080> and start with the sample photos.
